💡 TIPS FOR BETTER PROMPTS

✨ Be Specific: More details = better results. Choose style, character type, and accessories.

🎨 Style Matters: Different styles work best for different products. Notebook = minimalist, Coloring = line art, T-Shirt = bold graphics, 3D = renders.

👗 Accessories Add Personality: Choose gender first (for neutral characters) to see accessory options. Mix and match for unique looks!

🎲 Use Randomize: Stuck? Click RANDOMIZE for inspiration and unexpected combinations.

📋 Word Limit: Keep prompts under 120 words for best AI generation results.
